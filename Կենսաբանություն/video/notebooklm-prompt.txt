Make this video in ARMENIAN (Հայերեն). The audience is school students aged 11-15 (Yeghvard school No. 2), so use simple, friendly language, short sentences, and explain every medical word the first time it appears.

Follow this order:
1. What the circulatory system is (heart, vessels, blood) - one short intro.
2. The heart and its 4 chambers, the valves, how often it beats.
3. The two loops of circulation (lungs loop and body loop).
4. Arteries, veins, capillaries - how thick they are, and why veins are drawn blue in diagrams although blood is always red.
5. What blood is made of: plasma, red cells, white cells, platelets.
6. Blood groups (A, B, AB, O and Rh), who can give blood to whom, how to find out your own group.
7. Pulse, blood pressure and the ECG (what P, QRS and T mean; what a flat line means).
8. Cholesterol (LDL "bad", HDL "good") and vitamins/iron for blood.
9. How a wound stops bleeding (4 steps).
10. Healthy-heart habits, then a quick 3-question quiz for the viewers.

Use only the facts from the sources. Do not diagnose or give treatment advice; for pain or serious symptoms tell the viewer to talk to an adult or a doctor, and to call 112 in an emergency. Visual style: clean and bright, colours red, white and blue. Length: about 8-10 minutes.
